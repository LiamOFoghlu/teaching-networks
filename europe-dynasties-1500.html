Dynastic Europe c. 1500

Marriages, alliances, dynastic unions and vassalage among European states. Click a state to isolate its ties; use the legend to filter.

Snapshot spans roughly 1494–1502, so a few ties (e.g. Margaret Tudor's and Lucrezia Borgia's marriages) fall just after 1500. Positions are approximate. Ties between states are in practice ties between ruling houses; many smaller polities are omitted.
