Dynastic Europe c. 1500

Marriages, alliances, dynastic unions and vassalage among European states. Click a state to isolate its ties; use the legend to filter.

Snapshot spans roughly 1494–1502, so a few ties (e.g. Margaret Tudor's and Lucrezia Borgia's marriages) fall just after 1500. Positions are approximate. Ties between states are in practice ties between ruling houses; many smaller polities are omitted. Centrality is computed on the ties currently shown, treating them as undirected and counting several ties between the same pair once. Betweenness is normalised to 0–1; closeness is harmonic closeness, which still works when hiding some tie types cuts a node off.
