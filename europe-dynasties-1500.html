Dynastic Europe, 1200–1600

Marriages, alliances, dynastic unions and vassalage among European states, one snapshot per century. Click a state to isolate its ties; use the legend to filter.

Each snapshot is approximate: ties are dated within a few years of the century mark, and a few fall just after it. Positions are roughly geographic. Ties between states are in practice ties between ruling houses; wars and rivalries are not shown, and many smaller polities are omitted. Centrality is computed on the ties currently shown, treating them as undirected and counting several ties between the same pair once. Betweenness is normalised to 0–1; closeness is harmonic closeness, which still works when some states are cut off.

This network was generated by Claude (Anthropic) from its general historical knowledge, with selected facts checked against Wikipedia and Encyclopedia.com. It is a teaching sketch rather than a research dataset, so errors are possible.
